The Insight Behind Flex

As a company grows, so too should their funding options.

For a company compounding quickly, a priced round sells a permanent claim on everything that comes after. The faster the company grows, the more that claim turns out to be worth, and the more expensive that equity turns out to be.

Flex Capital was built around that reality. It gives founders a way to fund what’s next without taking on new dilution at the wrong moment, and without the covenants and constraints of traditional venture debt. It’s designed to sit cleanly within an existing cap table, integrating alongside prior investors without forcing a valuation conversation the company isn’t ready to have.
