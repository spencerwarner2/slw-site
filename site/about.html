About SLW

An independent growth-capital firm, built differently.

SLW provides flexible, bespoke capital solutions across the company lifecycle. We exist to solve the gap between what traditional investment playbooks offer and what high-growth companies need in today’s market, through flexible structures, deep partnership, and a long-term view of value creation.

Shawn O’Neill

Managing Partner

LinkedIn

Shawn O’Neill has over three decades of experience in the technology sector, where he has gained expertise in navigating the diverse and complex challenges faced by rapidly growing tech companies. He is the Managing Partner of SLW, a strategy he founded within Silver Lake in 2011. Shawn focuses on helping companies succeed by guiding them through critical inflection points, which include assisting with financing strategies, growth initiatives, scaling operations, and adapting to market shifts.

Drawing on deep industry knowledge and informed judgment built over his years as an investment banker, CFO, and investor, Shawn has earned the trust of CEOs who rely on his expertise and integrity for business-shaping decisions. His extensive network of respected relationships within and beyond the technology industry allows him to connect companies with the right resources, partners, and opportunities to drive meaningful growth.

Shawn remains a Senior Advisor at Silver Lake. Before founding SLW, Shawn launched Bandon Partners, a boutique investment bank, and served as CFO for two venture-backed technology companies. Earlier in his career, he was an investment banker with Morgan Stanley, DMG Technology Group, and Lehman Brothers—advising category-shaping companies.

Shawn holds a B.B.A. with honors from the University of Texas and serves on the Dean’s Advisory Board of the McCombs School of Business.

Michael Noryko

Managing Director

LinkedIn

Mike Noryko is a seasoned growth investor with a deep understanding of the technology shaping the industry. As a Managing Director at SLW, he leverages his market expertise and network to help companies scale and succeed. With two decades of experience in high-growth technology investments, Mike brings a unique perspective from his buy-side expertise and deep industry insight.

Before joining SLW nearly a decade ago, Mike spent 10 years as a public technology investor, including roles at Citadel and Andor Capital Management, where he led investments in many of the early SaaS IPOs. More than an investor, Mike is a trusted partner and connector, providing founders access to strategic relationships, resources, and market insights. He is committed to helping companies secure the right financial structures to drive transformation and create lasting value.

Mike attended UC Berkeley, where he played NCAA Division I water polo and graduated from the Haas School of Business.
