The Problem We Seek to Solve

For four decades, there has been virtually
no innovation in growth-stage financing.

Companies stay private longer and create enormous value, yet the way they fund growth hasn’t changed: round after round of priced equity, each one expensive, dilutive, and disconnected from the next. We saw an opportunity to do something different.

The faster you grow, the more a priced round ends up costing. Flex’s cost stays comparatively flat, funding what’s next without selling a permanent slice of equity.

Flex Capital

A better way
to fundraise.

Flex is SLW’s proprietary structure. As your company grows, so too should your options beyond traditional equity or venture debt. Flex lets you use capital now and return it later, without pricing a round or taking on new covenants. Like raising your Series C without being bogged down by price.
