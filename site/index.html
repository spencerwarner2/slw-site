Our History

SLW was founded in 2012 as Silver Lake Waterman, a later-stage growth strategy within Silver Lake, the global leader in technology investing. In 2024, we split out as an independent firm, while continuing to maintain a close relationship with Silver Lake.

Early on, we saw that funding options for companies were not dynamic enough for the massive and complex venture landscape of today. In response, we developed an innovative investment approach that solves company needs as they evolve over time.

Could we find a way to?

We started by imagining how we could solve company needs across their lifecycle.

Welcome to Lifecycle Capital.
