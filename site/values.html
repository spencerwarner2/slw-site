Our Values

01

Partnership over transactions

We treat every investment as the start of a relationship, not a single check. We’re a strategic, long-term partner on every deal.

02

Flexibility by design

The structure follows the company, not the other way around. We build each investment around the specific situation, stage, and cap table.

03

Discipline of credit

We bring the downside discipline of credit to growth investing, patient, and willing to wait for the right entry point rather than the nearest one.

04

A steady hand

We know how to help in both good times and bad. We’re a known quantity, a reliable partner even when markets tighten or conditions shift.
