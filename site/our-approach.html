Our Approach

A different kind of

Private markets are evolving faster than the playbooks built to navigate them. SLW was built for the gap those playbooks leave behind, structuring capital around the needs of the company, rather than the needs of the playbook.

The Problem We Seek to Solve

The old model hasn’t kept pace with how companies grow. So we started with a blank sheet of paper and rethought everything.

The Challenges

Every round brings
different needs.

Companies raise multiple rounds over their lifecycle, each time with different needs and market conditions. Company situations aren’t a straight line, and the cost of capital and industry dynamics are always evolving around them.

Rethinking Growth Capital

Could we find a
better way?

Over the last 15 years, we set out to imagine what an entirely new and holistic approach to growth-stage capital might look like, one that could lower the cost of capital, avoid pricing the company, flex to the need, and break the one-and-done cycle for good.

Why SLW

A more flexible way
to fund growth.

The traditional playbook asks companies to fit their needs into a fixed structure, a priced round or a rigid loan, on someone else’s terms. SLW starts from the opposite place: we build capital around the company and how it actually grows. Here’s how that approach compares to the way growth has always been financed.
